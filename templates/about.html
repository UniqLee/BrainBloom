About BrainBloom

BrainBloom is a peer chatroom and AI-powered study assistant designed to make learning interactive, fun, and personalized. Whether you're tackling a tough topic or just want to quiz yourself, BrainBloom has the tools to help you grow.

Key Features:

  • Peer Chatroom: Connect and discuss topics with other learners in real-time.
  • AI Study Assistant (Bloomie): Ask Bloomie for explanations, quizzes, and learning resources tailored to your needs.
  • Topic Tracking: Save, manage, and revisit all your study topics in one place.
  • Quiz Creation: Create and take quizzes to test your understanding and retention.
  • Prompt Sharing: Share study prompts with Bloomie from your chat history for instant AI assistance.

Our Mission:

BrainBloom aims to make learning collaborative and enjoyable. By combining peer interaction with AI guidance, we help learners understand complex topics, stay motivated, and track their progress over time.
